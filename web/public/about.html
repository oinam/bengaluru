About

An isometric pixel-art map of Bengaluru, drawn entirely by AI code from open map data. No photographs or satellite images appear in it.

The geography is real: roads, railways, the metro, lakes, building footprints, and most building heights come from the sources below. Ordinary buildings are drawn from a small set of types, so tree positions, which trees are in flower, roof types, roof colors, and windows are illustrative.

The map keeps Bengaluru's hours and seasons. It darkens as the sun sets over the city, wherever you are reading it, and lights its windows and street lamps; which windows are lit is made up. Its tabebuia, jacaranda, and gulmohar flower in their usual months, and from July to December none of the three does. Settings can hold the map at day or night and show any month.

The metro's lines are drawn over the map: solid where trains run, dashed where a line is being built, and dotted where one is only proposed. All three are as OpenStreetMap's mappers have recorded them. A proposed line is a plan, and its route and stations can still change.

Cost

This map cost nothing to make beyond an Anthropic subscription: AI writes the code, the data is open, one Mac draws it, and the hosting fits within free tiers. The richer-looking isometric maps of other cities are painted tile by tile by an image model on rented GPUs. We start at zero cost. If the map is worth making better, or if people are willing to contribute GPU compute, we will take that up; write to Brajeshwar.

Help improve this map

If you know Bengaluru, you can help. Use "Suggest a correction" on the map to say what is wrong or missing near you, and how you know.
