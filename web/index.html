    Good to know

    • Buildings, roofs, and trees are drawn from open data, so details may be wrong. Use Suggest a correction at the bottom right to report one.
    • Drag the Go to panel by its top bar to move it; double-click the bar to put it back.
    • Water and other animation, and the metro lines, can be turned off in Settings (⚙).
    • The map keeps Bengaluru's hours and seasons: it darkens at dusk and lights its windows and street lamps, and its trees flower in their months. Settings (⚙) can hold it at day or night, or show another month.
    • A metro line is solid where trains run, dashed where it is being built, and dotted where it is only proposed. Solid and dashed lines are faint underground.
